# Metric controls

Known damage in, what the published metrics say about it out. Every row is a synthetic system built from the paired audio of a fixed, language-stratified slice of the set (360 items over 15 conditions; set `f5a6420d67b7ef45`) and scored by the same code as a real system. Columns are read on the `clean` items of the slice, where the reference is the input and a defect is the only thing that happened; `lost_pct` is the share of speech blocks kept below -10 dB, `mag_snr` the signal-to-error ratio of the magnitude spectrum, `nv_lost_pct` the share of vocal sounds outside speech lost on the clean recordings of laughs, breaths and sighs, `fidelity` the published axis, and babble suppression is read at +4 dB. A delay of up to 100 ms, a flipped polarity and a gain of up to 12 dB are conventions the scorer undoes, not damage. The last three columns are the same-room block's rows, read on its items from two renders of the control, of the mixture and of the reference, as the board reads every system (`scoreevents.py`): the noise left over the labelled events and pauses, in dB, and the voice's level with the noise against without; a slice without the block leaves them blank. Generated by `scripts/controls.py`; the expectations live in its `EXPECTED` table.

## Blind spots

- `delete20ms`: retention is read per 100 ms block and as a 10th percentile: a dropped 20-80 ms (a consonant, a syllable onset) moves neither; only NISQA, waveform SI-SDR and the magnitude ratio react, the last from 40 ms on (a 20 ms hole stays above its ceiling). A recogniser's transcript does not react either: Whisper large-v3 restores the word from context for holes up to 160 ms (scorecontent.py's ctl_ rows), so the content check sees a dropout only from about 320 ms.
- `delete40ms`: as delete20ms.
- `delete80ms`: as delete20ms; `lost_pct` starts to see it when the span empties a block.
- `hfinvented`: a top octave replaced by noise of the same energy keeps every band level, the speaker embedding and NISQA; waveform SI-SDR reacts but cannot tell this from a harmless re-synthesis (`resynth`), and the magnitude ratio moves little, because the top octave holds little of the energy. Whether listeners hear it is an open listening question.
